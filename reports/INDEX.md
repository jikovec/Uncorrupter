## Report Rules

- Keep reports factual, source-backed, and dated.
- Record exact commands/checks and distinguish passed, failed, blocked, unavailable, not applicable, and not run.
- Do not put secrets, credentials, private paths, private media details, account identifiers, or private URLs in reports.
- A report is evidence, not a substitute for updating current canonical documentation.
- Historical reports should remain historically accurate even when later work supersedes them.
